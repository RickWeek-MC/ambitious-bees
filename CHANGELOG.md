# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased] - 0.1.0

First public version.

### Added
- 44 career bees for Forestry: Community Edition, with their own mutation tree, effects and overlays.
- Flower Pedestal: holds one item that "pedestal" bee effects work on.
- Bee-Bee Gun: fires the effect of a loaded queen in front of you, powered by honey.
- Mutation Frame, Eternal Frame and Poison Frame, plus Yin and Yang Essence.
- Honey-Comb Ingot (made by the Honey-Smelter bee).
- Creative tab with every item and every bee of the mod.
- Common config (`config/ambitious_bees-common.toml`): turn single effects off, protect players, pets and
  villagers, claim-mod friendly block changes, Mad Scientist block damage, Temporal bee TPS budget.
